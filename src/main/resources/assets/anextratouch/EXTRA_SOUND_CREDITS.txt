Playback gain matches Vintage Resourcify: its event gain of 4.0 times the vanilla
UI source gain of 0.25 equals AET's event/source gain of 1.0, before user volumes.
Both item.delete_partial and item.delete_all use this recording at that gain.

Modern event names are retained. AET shades MCLib 0.3.7.8 and requests every modern
event it uses from Minecraft 1.21.10 through AssetDirector. The client downloads
and caches the required files from Mojang; no modern recordings or Minecraft jars
are bundled. EFR remains optional and its available events are preferred. Missing
or failed downloads use local fallback events instead.
MCLib source: https://github.com/makamys/MCLib/tree/0.3.7.8
MCLib license notices: licenses/mclib-license.md, licenses/mclib-unlicense.txt,
licenses/mclib-lgpl-2.1.txt, and licenses/mclib-sloppydeploader-mit.txt.
CGLIB uses licenses/cglib-apache-2.0.txt; its NOTICE and asm-license.txt are retained.
The machine-readable mapping is extra_sound_fallbacks.json.
